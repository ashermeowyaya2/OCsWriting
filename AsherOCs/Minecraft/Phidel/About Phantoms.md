<font color="#245bdb"><center><h2>Phantoms (Normal State)</h2></center></font>

---

<h3><center>Appearance and Anatomy</center></h3>

40 cm in length
80 cm in width (including wings.)
15 cm in width (excluding wings.)
^ Current measurement, done from the top of my head, i might be completely wrong, plus i want to add their minimum and maximum.

They have 3 pit organs, much like some species of snakes, it helps them detect the body heat of prey. ( Which is why they show up around humans after they haven't slept for 72hrs, it increases the body heat. )
Oddly enough though, Phantoms do not have a digestive or respiratory system.
They do have a mouth, but it's purely to screech.
They have thinner skin, not only does it make them very sensitive to the sun, its possible to see parts of their skeleton, especially the thorax.
Phantom wings are messed up, sort of ripped because of some past rot (it does not spread any further).
They have a tail, which helps them balance in the air, and to stay up for longer periods of times.
The eyes of a Phantom are bright green, and they glow ! ( fun fact, if you manage to throw invisibility at a Phantom, their eyes are still visible).
Their vision is not different from a human's.
They have a hard time hearing things ! you could imagine their ears to be much like of a snake's. Which is with a hole near their eyes, see [] for some info. *NOTE: THIS DOES NOT MEAN THEY WORK **EXACTLY** HOW SNAKES DO.*
They do have neurons, and a brain, as to how it works and doesn't die, is a mystery.. given they won't get any oxygen or calories in. The best explanation would be is that it's also undead.

Phantoms are undead. Implying they cannot reproduce or die of old age.

---

<h3><center>Behaviour</center></h3>

They cannot stop moving, they're basically always flying. If they were to stop moving, they wouldn't die, the only reason a Phantom may stop, would be because their wings are harmed.
Phantoms can't sleep
They're mostly aggressive creatures, emphasis in mostly. Given they do not need to eat, they don't have much of a reason to kill other than for the fun of it.
