Phidel can have the habit of being unable to stay still, they'll often also prefer flight for navigation. Which is also why they often sit down! It helps them stay in place. Like the seat has the power to keep them still and stuck.
(*given some Minecraft limitations, I'd rather stay walking often, as using the elytra is still something I'm trying to get more used to.*)
They may also often fly around other Phantoms, or even other people around. Not to hurt or anything of the sorts, they're just keeping an eye for a good while...
Phidel unfortunately is used to watching their kind dying, burning in the sun, though, slaughtering Phantoms in front of them will still make them very upset. They won't lash out, but they will be weary of people.
Phidel is still a fully fledged Phantom, the difference in appearance is purely because of a trinket they have, given by [[OCs/Minecraft/Adelyn/|Adelyn]].
They have some scars around their body: burns, and scratches. (*i know the reference doesn't have them, it is by now a little outdated anyway*) Burns from the sun, and scratches from attempting to hide under trees when still in their usual phantom form.
I like to think in some ways, Phidel would have similar features to [[OCs/Minecraft/Adelyn/|Adelyn]], or genes generally. The eyes, nose and hair would be a few small differences from her. Eyes are more alike from their phantom form: a little big but still somewhat rectangular horizontally. Adelyn has slightly wider eyes, quadratic even ! Nose would still have a slight hint of Adelyn's curved(? not sure how to describe noses) nose, but it is mostly going inwards( should probably make them in The sims..). As for the hair, the texture is a slight mix from Adelyn's flowy straight hair, and the curly locks from [[OCs/Minecraft/Leonard/|Leonard]].. She took some samples when he was sleeping. (Look at [[Phidel's Trinket Composition]] to know a little more of how it would work).
